Where every number comes from.

Thirty-nine numbered references, each labelled by how far we checked it. Links open the publisher or the copy we read. Checked 9 to 12 October 2026; a link can still change.

How the engine uses them

01 How we label

Three labels.

  • Verified Read at the publisher or its official record.
  • Secondary Read through a mirror, a news report or a search summary.
  • Cited Cited in concept note v3 (sources checked 9 October 2026). Not re-opened in the 12 October check.

02 Wording we chose

Four places where we chose our words with care.

  • “USDA cut its forecast by half.” The 51% is a cut against USDA’s earlier forecast of 4.5 Mt, not a fall against last year.
  • Insects. The Kenyan storage-loss study counts losses to two insects. It does not rank insects against moisture or mould, and its authors say its total looks high. We do not quote the total.
  • Hermetic bags. About one in six Kenyan maize farmers uses them: a focus-group estimate of farmers, not a household count 2.
  • White maize. The tables are for US corn. A study of US white and waxy corn found each type needs its own curve 36. That is not Kenyan white maize either.
