1. Record

  • The logo was supplied on 12 October 2026 as a PNG ("Harvestly Grain Monitoring Logo.png"): a leaf-and-hand symbol with a gauge arc, beside the wordmark "Harvestly".
  • A vector trace was made the same day: harvestly-full, harvestly-horizontal and harvestly-symbol, each in a normal and a -reversed version for dark backgrounds (SVG, in the site brand folder). The deck uses PNG renders of the horizontal logo.
  • The earlier brief to a designer is withdrawn. The text "Harvestly" used as a placeholder in the pitch kit has been replaced by the logo.
  • Spelling: Harvestly, capital H, one word.

3. Brand usage

  • Which file: normal version on light backgrounds, -reversed on deep green or other dark backgrounds. Use the horizontal logo by default, the symbol alone where space is tight (avatar, favicon).
  • Clear space: keep free space equal to the height of the capital "H" of the wordmark on all four sides.
  • Minimum size: symbol alone 16 px high; horizontal logo 96 px wide.
  • One-colour: if colour is not possible, use all-black or all-white (reversed on dark). Never mix a one-colour mark with partial colour. Check it still reads when photocopied.
  • Do not: redraw, crop, stretch or rotate; change or swap colours; add gradients, shadows or outlines; rearrange the symbol and wordmark; retype the wordmark in another font; place it on busy photos or low-contrast backgrounds; add words inside or touching it.
  • No endorsement line. The logo is never combined with a company name or logo. The vector files here are the team's own trace of the supplied PNG; treat them as the master and do not trace again.
